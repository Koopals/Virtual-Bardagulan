# Virtual Office

A pixel-hotel style virtual office that runs in the browser. Real people open the same link, set up their character, and see, hear and chat with each other in real time.

## What you can do

- **Set up your character** on your first visit: name, role, skin tone, 14 hairstyles (pigtails, braid, bob, afro and more), 12 outfits (dresses, blouses, blazers, overalls and more), pants, shorts or skirts, 15 kinds of headwear (crown, tiara, flower crown, cat ears, hijab, cowboy hat and more), and extras. Your browser remembers it.
- **Hero costumes:** 10 original superhero costumes inspired by the Avengers, like Star Captain with a shield on his back, Red Armor, and Thunder God with a cape and hammer. They are original designs, not official Marvel costumes.
- **Talk with live voice:** unmute and people near you hear you. Voices get quieter with distance and fade out about 10 tiles away.
- **See everyone live:** where people walk and sit, their status, emotes, what they're eating or drinking, and who's talking.
- **Clean screen:** the office fills the whole window. Round buttons in the top-right corner open dropdown menus: your profile (status, mic, speaker and actions), 🎤 mic on/off, 👥 Team, ✅ Tasks and 🎵 Music. A red dot shows when something new happened, like someone joining, a new task for you or a song starting. The chat lives in a 💬 bubble in the bottom-right corner, like Facebook Messenger, with a red unread count.
- **Chat, reply and mention:** messages to the whole room, or a private whisper to one person (`/w Name hi`). Hover a message and click **↩** to reply to it. Type **@** to pick someone to mention, or use `@everyone`. The person you mention or reply to gets a sound and a pop-up.
- **Soundproof meeting room:** inside the Harbour room, only people inside hear your voice and get your chat messages. Everyone else sees `· · ·`.
- **Lock a room:** click **🔒 Lock room** in the top-left panel and choose a passcode. People who join later need it.
- **Do things together:** emoji reactions, 30 dances, including 4 Pinoy TikTok dances (Ikembot Mo, Ale fit check, Budots, Dr. Beat) and 6 TikTok viral dances (Pikki Pikki, Renegade, Toosie Slide, Stanky Leg, Out West, Bear Shake), and you can invite someone to dance with you. **Carry people:** open someone's card → 🥊 Fight → 🤲 Pick up, walk around with them over your head, then 🚀 Throw them (they land flat for 5 seconds, or splash into the pool) or ⬇ Put down. If you're the one being carried, click 🤸 Wiggle free 3 times, slaps with a trout, treating everyone to coffee or pizza, and touching the tree, presents, corgi, plants, lamp, whiteboard, bell and laptops.
- **Shared music:** the office radio and YouTube / YouTube Music links play for everyone in the room. Songs from your own computer only play for you.
- **One-click music:** the 🎵 Music menu has tabs for **YouTube** (24/7 lofi radios, hits, love songs, classics, Christmas), **YouTube Music** (song radios that keep playing similar songs), **Spotify** (18 Spotify playlists; full songs need Spotify Premium logged in, otherwise 30-second previews) and **⭐ My picks** (press ⭐ Save on anything that's playing to keep it for one-click listening). With a YouTube key on the server you can also search any song.
- **Play any link for the room:** the jukebox plays YouTube, YouTube Music, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Spotify, SoundCloud, Twitch, Google Drive videos, direct video files (`.mp4`, `.webm`) and most other embeddable links. Click **⛶** on the player for the big screen and have a movie night. Late joiners jump to the right spot in direct video files. Some sites, like Netflix, don't allow playing inside other pages. Songs from your own computer only play for you.
- **Office, hallway and rooms:** you start in the office, with a hallway (half the office's size) right in front of it, so you see both at once. Walk through the opening in the low wall to get from one to the other. The hallway is a lobby lounge with sofas, armchairs, coffee tables, rugs, lamps, plants, art, a room directory, a vending machine and a water cooler. The hallway has a row of glowing doors, one for each room: game room, pool, basketball court, sleeping quarters, town hall, bar, gym and beach. Hover over a door to see which room it is and how many people are inside, then click it to go in. Each room is as big as the office (with windows along the back) and shows on its own. Use the room's door to come back, or the **Rooms** buttons to jump anywhere. People only show up, and can only hear each other, when they're in the same area. Chat still reaches everyone.
- **Entertainment wing:** a hallway runs through the east wing, with a door into each room: game room, pool and basketball court on one side, sleeping quarters, town hall and bar on the other, and the gym at the end with a door out to the beach.
- **Band stage (Bar room):** a drum kit, keyboard, electric guitar with amp, and a DJ set. Click an instrument and your character walks over and plays it: drummers sit behind the kit with sticks, the keyboard player stands at the keys, the guitarist grabs the guitar off its stand, and the DJ works the decks with one hand on the headphones. - **Choose your microphone:** open your avatar menu (top-right) and pick a mic under **🎙️ Microphone**, for example your headset instead of Krisp. The office remembers your choice, switches right away even while you're talking, and falls back to your default mic if the chosen one is unplugged. Mic names show after you turn your mic on once.
- **Choose your speaker:** under **🔈 Speaker** in the same menu, pick where voice chat, office sounds and video files play (for example your headset instead of the Krisp speaker). YouTube and Spotify players always use your system default speaker. Works in Chrome and Edge.
- **Automatic updates:** when you upload a new version and Render redeploys, every open office shows **🔄 The office was updated. Reloading in 15 s…** and reloads itself, putting each person back where they were with the same mic and speaker settings. It waits while someone is typing, watching a movie, sending an image or carrying someone. **Later** snoozes it for 10 minutes. Everyone needs to reload by hand once to get this feature.
- **Voice through the server:** if your direct voice link with someone is blocked (common on work laptops, VPNs and office Wi-Fi), your voices travel through the office server instead, automatically. The 🎧 Voice list shows **via server** next to those people. It needs no setup; it adds a little delay and uses the server's bandwidth only while someone is talking. If you hear an echo, use a headset.
- **Muting:** muting your mic only stops others hearing you. You still hear everyone near you until you turn **🔈 Speaker** off. If the browser blocks voice audio from starting, a green **🔊 Click to hear people near you** button appears. If you've never allowed the microphone and can't hear some people on the same network, press **🎧 Allow once, stay muted** in the Voice list: you stay muted, but voice can connect.
- **🎧 Voice list:** the avatar menu also shows your voice connection with each person (can hear, too far, other room, connecting, blocked) and a **🔄 Reconnect** button. Voice connections now repair themselves when they drop or get stuck. If someone always shows **blocked**, your network (often a work laptop, VPN or office Wi-Fi) is blocking direct voice: add a TURN server as **ICE_SERVERS** on Render (see below), or try another network.
- **Sound stays in the room:** you only hear voice chat and sound effects (slaps, splashes, bells, games) from people in the same room as you. The Harbour meeting room and the hallway count as separate rooms. Music is not affected and keeps playing wherever you are.

- **Movie room removed:** the movie theater is gone; the 🏛️ Town hall (screen sharing on a giant screen) replaces it. The jukebox's ⛶ big-screen player still works for watching videos together. Anyone who was in the old movie room is moved to the hallway.

Stand up or walk around and you hear people near you again. The Team list shows **🎧 Focusing** next to people seated at their desks. ## Put it online (free, about 5 minutes)

GitHub Pages can only host files, and multiplayer needs a small server. Render can run that server for free straight from this repository.

1. Push this repository to GitHub (public or private).
2. Sign in at [render.com](https://render.com) with your GitHub account.
3. Click **New → Blueprint**, pick this repository, and click **Apply**. Render reads `render.yaml`, installs everything and starts the server.
4. When it says **Live**, open the address it gives you, like `https://virtual-office-xxxx.onrender.com`. That's your multiplayer office. Share it, or add `?room=yourteam`.

**Free plan limits:** Render puts a free service to sleep after 15 minutes with no visitors. The next visitor waits about a minute while it wakes up. While people are in the office, the page sends a small keep-alive message every few minutes so it stays awake.

## Song search (optional)

The 🔍 search in the 🎵 Music menu (YouTube and YouTube Music tabs) shows results right inside the office: press ▶ to play a song for everyone or ＋ to add it to the queue. On the YouTube Music tab, ▶ starts a song radio. Search needs a free YouTube Data API key on the server:

1. Go to [console.cloud.google.com](https://console.cloud.google.com), create a project, open **APIs & Services → Library**, and turn on **YouTube Data API v3**.
2. Open **APIs & Services → Credentials → Create credentials → API key**, and copy the key.
3. On Render, open your service, go to **Environment**, add **YOUTUBE_API_KEY** with that key, and save. Render restarts the server.

The free daily quota allows about 100 searches; results are cached for 30 minutes. If a search fails, open **Logs** on Render: the server prints Google's reason (for example, the API isn't turned on or the key is wrong). YouTube Music has no separate key; the same key covers both tabs.

## Images in the chat

Paste an image (**Ctrl+V**) into the chat box or the bottom bar, or click **🖼️** to pick a file. You see a preview, can add a caption, and press **Send**. Big pictures are shrunk to 1280 px before sending. It works with whispers, replies and the Harbour meeting room like any other message, and people can click an image to open it full size.

Images are kept on the server in memory for a day (they disappear sooner if the free Render server restarts). No setting is needed. Only PNG, JPG, WebP and GIF files are accepted, and only images from your own office server are shown in the chat.

### Optional: a TURN server

Voice already falls back to going through the office server when direct voice is blocked, so you don't need this. A TURN server is an optional alternative relay with lower delay:

1. Sign up free at [metered.ca](https://www.metered.ca/tools/openrelay/) (Open Relay: 20 GB a month free).
2. In the Metered dashboard, open **TURN Server**, create a credential and copy the **credentials link**. It looks like `https://<yourapp>.metered.live/api/v1/turn/credentials?apiKey=<key>`.
3. On Render, open your service → **Environment** → add **TURN_API_URL** with that link, and save. Render restarts.
4. Everyone reloads the office (Ctrl+F5). The 🎧 Voice list shows **relay** next to people connected through TURN.

You can also set **ICE_SERVERS** by hand (JSON list of STUN/TURN servers) instead.

## Room passcodes

There are two ways to lock a room:

- **From inside the office:** click **🔒 Lock room** in the top-left panel and choose a passcode (3+ characters). Everyone already inside stays in and can reconnect without it. Anyone inside can click **🔓 Unlock room**. These passcodes are kept in the server's memory, so **they reset when the server restarts or falls asleep** on Render's free plan.
- **Permanent passcodes, set on the server:** in Render, open your service → **Environment** → **Add Environment Variable**, then save. The server restarts with the new setting.

| Variable | Example | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `OFFICE_PASSWORD` | `blue-tiger-42` | One passcode for every room |
| `ROOM_PASSWORDS` | `{"sjp":"blue-tiger-42","leads":"orange-fox"}` | Passcodes for specific rooms. Wins over `OFFICE_PASSWORD`. |

People type the passcode once, and their browser remembers it. After 5 wrong tries the connection is closed, and after 10 wrong tries from the same network, that network has to wait 10 minutes.

## Live voice

- **How it works:** voice goes directly between people's browsers (WebRTC). The server only helps them find each other; it never carries or records audio.
- **Who hears you:** your browser only sends your voice to people within earshot (about 10 tiles) and on the same side of the meeting room glass.
- **Controls:** **M** or the mic button mutes you. **Speaker off** silences everyone for you. The volume slider sets how loud people are.
- **Corporate networks:** by default it uses Google's free STUN servers, which work on most home and office networks. Some strict company networks block direct connections; there, voice won't connect but everything else still works. To fix that, add a TURN server in Render's **Environment** settings:

  `ICE_SERVERS` = `[{"urls":"stun:stun.l.google.com:19302"},{"urls":"turn:turn.example.com:3478","username":"user","credential":"pass"}]`

- **Size:** voice works best with up to about 20 people in a room. Each person connects directly to everyone else.

## Run it on your own computer

You need [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 18 or newer.

```bash
npm install
npm start
```

Then open http://localhost:3000 in two browser windows to see two people in the office. Use headphones when testing voice on one computer.

## Good to know

- **It's not end-to-end encrypted.** Chat goes through your server. Voice is encrypted between browsers, as all WebRTC audio is. Don't use it for passwords, customer data or anything confidential.
- **Meeting room privacy:** the server only sends meeting-room chat to people inside. Each speaker's browser stops sending voice to people outside.
- **Room size:** a room holds up to 40 people. Set `MAX_PER_ROOM` to change it.
