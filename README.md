# Virtual Office

A pixel-hotel style virtual office that runs in the browser. Real people open the same link, set up their character, and see, hear and chat with each other in real time.

## What you can do

- **Set up your character** on your first visit: name, role, skin tone, 14 hairstyles (pigtails, braid, bob, afro and more), 12 outfits (dresses, blouses, blazers, overalls and more), pants, shorts or skirts, 15 kinds of headwear (crown, tiara, flower crown, cat ears, hijab, cowboy hat and more), and extras. Your browser remembers it.
- **Hero costumes:** 10 original superhero costumes inspired by the Avengers, like Star Captain with a shield on his back, Red Armor, and Thunder God with a cape and hammer. They are original designs, not official Marvel costumes.
- **Talk with live voice:** unmute and people near you hear you. Voices get quieter with distance and fade out about 10 tiles away.
- **See everyone live:** where people walk and sit, their status, emotes, what they're eating or drinking, and who's talking.
- **Chat, reply and mention:** messages to the whole room, or a private whisper to one person (`/w Name hi`). Hover a message and click **↩** to reply to it. Type **@** to pick someone to mention, or use `@everyone`. The person you mention or reply to gets a sound and a pop-up.
- **Soundproof meeting room:** inside the Harbour room, only people inside hear your voice and get your chat messages. Everyone else sees `· · ·`.
- **Lock a room:** click **🔒 Lock room** in the top-left panel and choose a passcode. People who join later need it.
- **Do things together:** emoji reactions, 20 dances (invite someone to dance with you), slaps with a trout, treating everyone to coffee or pizza, and touching the tree, presents, corgi, plants, lamp, whiteboard, bell and laptops.
- **Shared music:** the office radio and YouTube / YouTube Music links play for everyone in the room. Songs from your own computer only play for you.
- **Play any link for the room:** the jukebox plays YouTube, YouTube Music, Vimeo, Dailymotion, Spotify, SoundCloud, Twitch, Google Drive videos, direct video files (`.mp4`, `.webm`) and most other embeddable links. Click **⛶** on the player for the big screen and have a movie night. Late joiners jump to the right spot in direct video files. Some sites, like Netflix, don't allow playing inside other pages. Songs from your own computer only play for you.
- **Office, hallway and rooms:** you start in the office, with a hallway (half the office's size) right in front of it, so you see both at once. Walk through the opening in the low wall to get from one to the other. The hallway is a lobby lounge with sofas, armchairs, coffee tables, rugs, lamps, plants, art, a room directory, a vending machine and a water cooler. The hallway has a row of glowing doors, one for each room: game room, pool, basketball court, sleeping quarters, bar, movie room, gym and beach. Hover over a door to see which room it is and how many people are inside, then click it to go in. Each room is as big as the office (with windows along the back) and shows on its own. Use the room's door to come back, or the **Rooms** buttons to jump anywhere. People only show up, and can only hear each other, when they're in the same area. Chat still reaches everyone.
- **Entertainment wing:** a hallway runs through the east wing, with a door into each room: game room, pool and basketball court on one side, sleeping quarters, bar and movie room on the other, and the gym at the end with a door out to the beach.
- **Movie room:** two rows of sofas facing a big screen. The screen shows what's playing, and clicking it opens the big-screen player for everyone's movie link.
- **Fitness gym:** treadmills, exercise bikes, a punching bag, a bench press, dumbbells and yoga mats. Your character works out on each one, and a long workout makes you walk faster for a minute.
- **Beach:** walk through the gym's east door onto a huge beach with palm trees, towels and umbrellas, sun loungers, a tiki bar, a bonfire, a lifeguard tower, a sandcastle, surfboards, a beach ball and crabs. Your character changes into a beach outfit as soon as you step on the sand, and back again when you come inside. Walk into the sea to swim.
- **Camera angle:** use the buttons on the right edge of the office view (⟲ ⟳ to rotate, 📐 to change the tilt, ⌂ to reset), right-drag to turn the view freely, or press Q / E to rotate, T to tilt and R to reset. Walls between you and the room fade out so you can always see inside.
- **Basketball matches:** when two or more people are on the court, they're split into Red and Blue teams and play a match on their own: passing, shooting, defending and rebounds. The scoreboard shows the score, and the first team to 21 wins.
- **Fight moves:** open someone's card and click **🥊 Fight** to slap, punch or kick them (your character walks up to them first), or throw a meteor, car, laptop, grenade, dinosaur, shield, hammer, cannon or wheelchair at them. The shield and hammer fly back to your hand. Three hits in a few seconds, or one big throw, knocks the person down for a moment. There's a training dummy in the gym to practise on.
- **🦽 Wheelchair:** turn it on under Extras in your look. Your character rides it everywhere (the wheels turn and you push the rims as you move) and parks it beside you when you sit on a seat or lie down.
- **🪑 Sit** with the button next to the emotes, or press 6: you sit on the nearest free seat, or on the floor if none is close. Click it again (🧍 Stand up) to get up.
- **Fuller rooms:** the game room has ping-pong, pool, foosball and air hockey tables, a gaming lounge with bean bags, more arcade cabinets, a board-game shelf and a dartboard. The pool room has a hot tub, a water slide, a rubber duck, more loungers, showers and a smoothie bar. The court has bleachers, a ball rack and a trophy cabinet. The sleeping quarters have 10 more beds, a reading nook and wardrobes. The bar has booths, a lounge, a DJ booth, a karaoke stage and high tables. The movie room is a cinema with a bigger screen, curtains, 36 more seats, aisle lights, posters and a snack counter. The gym has more treadmills, rowing machines, a squat rack, a boxing ring, a mirror wall, lockers and more yoga mats.
- **🛌 Lie down** anywhere with the button next to the emotes, or press 5. Click it again or walk away to get up.
- **🚬 Smoke break** reaction, and tabs at the top of the game panel to switch between board games.
- **Game room:** chess, checkers, reversi, Connect Four and tic-tac-toe on real tables. Play the computer (easy, normal or hard), two people on one screen, or sit down **online** against someone in the office. Click a table where two people are playing, or pick **👀 Watch**, to watch their match live. You can switch between live matches. There's also a Snake arcade cabinet.
- **Swimming pool:** click the water to jump in and swim. Lie on a sun lounger by the pool.
- **Sleeping quarters:** click a bed to lie down and nap.
- **Basketball court:** walk onto the court and your character plays by itself: dribbling, shooting and scoring. The scoreboard shows the top scorers. Walk off the court to stop.
- **Bar and dance floor:** order drinks, clink glasses (**🍻 Cheers**) and buy a round for everyone. After 4 beers you stumble off to bed and wake up later with a headache. Step onto the light-up dance floor to start dancing.
- **Slap fights:** get slapped 3 times in a few seconds and you fall over for a moment.
- **Rooms:** add `?room=yourteam` to the link to get a separate office. Use the **🔗 Copy invite link** button.

## Put it online (free, about 5 minutes)

GitHub Pages can only host files, and multiplayer needs a small server. Render can run that server for free straight from this repository.

1. Push this repository to GitHub (public or private).
2. Sign in at [render.com](https://render.com) with your GitHub account.
3. Click **New → Blueprint**, pick this repository, and click **Apply**. Render reads `render.yaml`, installs everything and starts the server.
4. When it says **Live**, open the address it gives you, like `https://virtual-office-xxxx.onrender.com`. That's your multiplayer office. Share it, or add `?room=yourteam`.

**Free plan limits:** Render puts a free service to sleep after 15 minutes with no visitors. The next visitor waits about a minute while it wakes up. While people are in the office, the page sends a small keep-alive message every few minutes so it stays awake.

## Room passcodes

There are two ways to lock a room:

- **From inside the office:** click **🔒 Lock room** in the top-left panel and choose a passcode (3+ characters). Everyone already inside stays in and can reconnect without it. Anyone inside can click **🔓 Unlock room**. These passcodes are kept in the server's memory, so **they reset when the server restarts or falls asleep** on Render's free plan.
- **Permanent passcodes, set on the server:** in Render, open your service → **Environment** → **Add Environment Variable**, then save. The server restarts with the new setting.

| Variable | Example | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| `OFFICE_PASSWORD` | `blue-tiger-42` | One passcode for every room |
| `ROOM_PASSWORDS` | `{"sjp":"blue-tiger-42","leads":"orange-fox"}` | Passcodes for specific rooms. Wins over `OFFICE_PASSWORD`. |

People type the passcode once, and their browser remembers it. After 5 wrong tries the connection is closed, and after 10 wrong tries from the same network, that network has to wait 10 minutes.

## Live voice

- **How it works:** voice goes directly between people's browsers (WebRTC). The server only helps them find each other; it never carries or records audio.
- **Who hears you:** your browser only sends your voice to people within earshot (about 10 tiles) and on the same side of the meeting room glass.
- **Controls:** **M** or the mic button mutes you. **Speaker off** silences everyone for you. The volume slider sets how loud people are.
- **Corporate networks:** by default it uses Google's free STUN servers, which work on most home and office networks. Some strict company networks block direct connections; there, voice won't connect but everything else still works. To fix that, add a TURN server in Render's **Environment** settings:

  `ICE_SERVERS` = `[{"urls":"stun:stun.l.google.com:19302"},{"urls":"turn:turn.example.com:3478","username":"user","credential":"pass"}]`

- **Size:** voice works best with up to about 20 people in a room. Each person connects directly to everyone else.

### GitHub Pages copy

There are no made-up coworkers anywhere. Everyone in the office is a real person.

- **The Render address** (`https://virtual-office-xxxx.onrender.com`) connects on its own. This is the easiest link to share.
- **The GitHub Pages address** starts out not connected, with just you in the office. Click **🔌 Connect to server** in the top-left panel and paste your Render address. Your browser remembers it.
- **To skip that step for everyone,** share a link with the server in it, like `https://<you>.github.io/<repo>/?server=virtual-office-xxxx.onrender.com`. The **🔗 Copy invite link** button builds this for you.
- **To build the address into the page itself,** edit this line near the top of `index.html`:

  ```js
  window.VO_SERVER = 'https://virtual-office-xxxx.onrender.com';
  ```

## Run it on your own computer

You need [Node.js](https://nodejs.org) 18 or newer.

```bash
npm install
npm start
```

Then open http://localhost:3000 in two browser windows to see two people in the office. Use headphones when testing voice on one computer.

## Good to know

- **It's not end-to-end encrypted.** Chat goes through your server. Voice is encrypted between browsers, as all WebRTC audio is. Don't use it for passwords, customer data or anything confidential.
- **Meeting room privacy:** the server only sends meeting-room chat to people inside. Each speaker's browser stops sending voice to people outside.
- **Room size:** a room holds up to 40 people. Set `MAX_PER_ROOM` to change it.
